# Phase 0 — Source Register

**Date:** 2026-11-12 · **Phase:** 0 (Research) · **Status:** Living register; findings reports cite these IDs (`S01`…).
**Method:** Every entry was retrieved during this session. Bibliographic metadata was cross-checked against Crossref (publisher-deposited records), the arXiv API, or the original repository/documentation. The **Access level** column states exactly what was inspected. An entry marked *metadata only* means that **no claim from the paper body is used** beyond what its title and venue establish; it is listed as a lead, not as evidence.

Access levels: `FULL` = full text or full source file inspected · `PARTIAL` = specific sections or files inspected · `ABSTRACT` = abstract only · `META` = bibliographic metadata only · `SPEC` = official specification page.

## Coverage summary

- **Distinct sources registered:** 42. Of these, **26 were inspected at `ABSTRACT` level or deeper**, including full text or source for S01, S02, S07, S15, S16, S19 and S32, plus official specs S21–S22. The remaining entries are clearly marked `META` leads.
- **Not found in the literature (an evidence gap, not an oversight):** a published, instrumented measurement of the **face-turning torque** of a standard 3×3 cube, and a peer-reviewed study of **parallel-jaw fingertip geometry for layer-selective cube turning**.
- **Access limitations:** IEEE Xplore pages render client-side and could not be read, so IEEE abstracts were obtained via Semantic Scholar. The Columbia Academic Commons copy of S35 was blocked by bot protection. No paywalled full text was accessed.
