### G2. The stiffness arithmetic leaves out the projection onto the face axis (should fix before the user decides GD7)
- FACT: ER1's probe 5 rotates the piece "about the face axis" (`er1_probe_magnet_stiffness.py` docstring and l.50,
  axis (0, 0, 1)).
- FACT: ER6's +377 / +370 / +369 are slide, cross and tangential stiffnesses, about axes perpendicular to the piece's
  radial axis.
- INFERENCE (geometry): a coaxial pair contributes nothing about its own axis. About the face axis it contributes
  sin²(angle) × k: 0.5 for an edge (45°) and 0.667 for a corner (54.7°). At scale 0.756 that is about
  0.5 × 285 ≈ **+142** (edge) and 0.667 × 279 ≈ **+186** (corner), not +285 / +279.
- The sums in l.162–164 and Summary item 5 (l.71–75) become:

  | Arm | Edge | Corner | Corner+edge pair |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | HC20, as drafted | −645 | −141 | **+144** |
  | HC20, corrected | about −788 | about −234 | **about −92** |

  So the one hopeful static claim ("HC20's corner+edge cluster might turn positive") probably does not hold under
  GD2 (a).
- At N52 under GD2 (b), the same arithmetic gives the pair about +230, the edge about −648 and the corner about −52.
- **Fix.** Correct G.2 and the Summary before asking the user about GD7. P2 computes the real values either way.

### G3. Under GD2 (a), HC20 is almost certain to fail signal 4, so the selection becomes HC8 or nothing (should fix)

  r2 = 8 mm.
- INFERENCE: B1 pieces only rotate about the centre (ball joints, no radial freedom). Every magnet's radius is
  therefore invariant, and that lower bound is reachable in dynamics. The rigid U-turn minimum is 8.02 mm, and W3's
  pieces tilt by 0.3–0.4°.
- The draft fixes in advance that weight-zero perpendicular flags count, which is correct practice. But HC20 is the arm
  with the least negative static estimate and about half the probe's CPU (3.2 of 6.6), and it would then fail signal 4
  almost surely. HC8 keeps the corner+edge cluster at about −234 (G2).
- Under GD2 (b), the 4×2 magnets at r = 28.06 mm give a hard **8.12 mm** bound in any motion, so this case cannot
  occur.
- **Fix.** Put this consequence in GD2 and GD3. Either run HC20 only under (b), or add a static stop in P1: stop an arm
  whose radius lower bound lies below r2.

### G4. GD2's "recommended default" (a) is a user decision, and the draft itself shows (b) is the version a G1 path needs (should fix)
