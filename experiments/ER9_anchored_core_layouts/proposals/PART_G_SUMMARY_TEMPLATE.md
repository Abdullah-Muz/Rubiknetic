### Limits (G.10)
- **Rehearsal only.** ER7's seeds have been used for diagnostics and for earlier selections, and this part reuses
  them again. Twenty rest seeds give wide intervals: 0/20 has a 95 % upper bound of 17 %.
- **P-58 abstraction:** magnets have no collision geometry and sit at real radii inside B1 pieces. B1 has no radial
  freedom, so magnetic preload, and with it the commercial use of repelling magnets, has no counterpart.
- **Force-law coverage.** CE: M5's 5°. PC on-path: P-60, plus P4.0 for PG8. PC off-path: D_val from P4.d, otherwise
  7.5°. X pairs: dipole only (INFERENCE: beyond r2, same-type pairs get no near-field correction either, so X pairs
  beyond 8 mm are treated as every pair is); near X pairs are counted as out of regime and were never validated.
- **Lateral offsets.** Corner–edge pairs crossing a turning boundary pass lateral offsets of 6–8 mm; V3a covers
  0–6 mm. M5 had the same gap at the same positions. Disclosed, not a new rule.
- **L0's own largest near tilt in ER7 was 12.59°** (FACT, ER7 SUMMARY), above CE's 5°. INFERENCE: it came from abuse
  trials (rule 3). [TODO before the probe is read: confirm from ER7's L0 records by sub-experiment.]
- **Numerics.** implicitfast at 0.5 ms has a bounded energy oscillation of about 10 % with core magnets (FACT, M6
  SUMMARY). The ideal-magnet caveat (up to 1.5×) carries over, and no derated arm is included.
- **Fit (U1).** Under F-A, Part G's geometry is L0's and C52's, not the CAD's. Commercial dimensions are unknown.
