## Interpretation (`INFERENCE`)
- At `noslip_iterations` 10, none of the three magnet layouts shows the rest slips that failed M5 and Part G's
  signal 2. This includes M5's own 48-magnet layout (L0), the simplest arm and the one with the lowest turning torque on
  the probe seeds (median 0.144 N·m, against 0.378 for PG8 and 0.576 for PG20).
- The result is conditional on the setting. MuJoCo describes NoSlip as an ad-hoc post-pass that "no longer solves a
  well-defined optimization problem".
  - Open MuJoCo issue #2423 reports that joint `frictionloss` together with `damping` and NoSlip makes a spinning joint
    decelerate far too slowly. Every cube piece joint has both
    (`docs/research/NOSLIP_AND_CUBE_FRICTION_FINDINGS.md`).
  - This cannot show at rest, but it could show in turning, overshoot and snap-back, which Stage 2 tests.
- Whether a physical cube creeps under these loads stays unverified. The contact friction of 0.2 has no primary
  source (same findings file).

## Review notes and limits
An independent read-only code review of D13 and D14 (a subagent, 2026-11-23) found nothing that changes a reported
number or decision. It made four low-severity points, recorded here:
1. **The contact-force check is circular for elliptic cones.** For elliptic cones, `mj_contactForce` returns
   `efc_force` itself, so `d13_run.check_contacts_against_mujoco` always gives 0. The check also does not test the
   `efc_vel` rows. The row layout rests on MuJoCo's documented convention, not on this check. This affects D13's
   reported-only creep share, not any rule.
2. **Cost projections after `--resume` omit earlier sessions' time.** D13's re-check left out about 0.3 h. The 4 h and
   6 h stops were not close.
3. **Capture before record.** A run's event capture is written before its record. A worker killed between the two
   writes would make `--resume` fail on the existing capture file. This did not occur.
4. **D13's S1 variant (reversed setup order) perturbs piece poses by only about 0.02°.** Its "not setup-linked" label
   tests sensitivity to a small perturbation, not the misalignment pattern itself.

**Limits** (as fixed in PROTOCOL section 6): rest only; the setting was chosen after the failures; physical
credibility is unverified. Twenty seeds per arm put the 95 % upper bound on a per-seed slip rate at about 17 %.
