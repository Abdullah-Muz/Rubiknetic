## Interpretation (`INFERENCE`)
1. **With NoSlip, no arm has a failing probe signal on the rehearsal seeds.** That includes M5's own 48-magnet layout
   (L0), which has no core magnets. This is the first configuration in the project where an arm has no failing probe
   signal. It is conditional on a setting chosen after the failures, and it is unscored.
2. **NoSlip makes L0 stickier but leaves it inside every limit.**
   - L0's median turn torque rose 34 % (prediction: within ±20 %; missed), its largest from 0.46 to 0.60 N·m, and its
     worst scored snap-back error from 0.12° to 0.29° (limit 0.5°).
   - This fits harder static friction holding a weakly restored layer slightly short of home, together with the mild
     extra coasting seen in the decay check. L0 has the smallest margin on snap-back.
3. **PG20's median torque fell 43 % (0.58 → 0.33 N·m), but its largest move rose to 0.97 N·m,** close to the 1 N·m
   clip. Not explained by these runs.
4. **PG8 and PG20 now block the largest forgiveness misalignments (+16° to +20°).** At the frozen setting they
   completed them; L0 already blocked many at the frozen setting. INFERENCE: harder friction resists a turn that starts
   far out of alignment. Blocking is allowed, and arguably physical.
5. **Against the realism reference (about 0.2 N·m) and the warning level (0.45–0.5 N·m)** (P-67 item 4; reported, not
   limits): L0's median (0.19 N·m) is at the reference, PG20's (0.33) is between the two, and PG8's (0.42) is near the
   warning level.

## Limits
