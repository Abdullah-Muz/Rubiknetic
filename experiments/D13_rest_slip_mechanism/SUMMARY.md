## Interpretation (`INFERENCE`; not tested beyond these runs)
1. **The slips are the end of a slow creep, and the creep is set mainly by the softness of MuJoCo's friction
   constraint.**
   - Making friction harder relative to the normal direction slowed the creep about 10 × (V1). Removing the
     regularization of the friction rows removed it (V2).
   - Where V1's creep still reached the threshold, the same jump followed, about ten times later.
   - This is the behaviour MuJoCo's documentation describes ("a persistent tangential load can produce a nonzero
     steady slip velocity").
2. **Gravity supplies much of the persistent load, together with the magnet torques.** It also decides where the
   slip happens: the U∩F row with gravity down, the D∩F row in the one flipped-gravity event, and no events
   without gravity. Why the front (F, −y) row rather than another side is not explained by these runs.
3. **The mechanism is not purely soft-constraint creep.**
   - Q4 shows a large share of sliding at the cone limit, and V1 and V3 still produce events.
   - Friction 0.2 with these loads leaves some contacts close to genuine sliding. Whether a physical cube with these
     magnets would creep at all is not addressed (section 6 of the protocol).
4. **The 0.2° slips that failed Part G's signal 2 for PG8 and PG20 come largely from the simulator's friction
   model, not from the magnet layouts.**
   - PG20 would plausibly have met signal 2 at a harder friction setting. Not shown: the snap-back, turning and
     forgiveness results have not been rerun at any other friction setting.
   - The harder setting also changes the turning trials, and could raise turning torque or cause stalls.

## Review notes (added 2026-11-23, after D14)
An independent read-only code review found nothing that changes a number or decision here. Two points bear on this
summary; the full list is in `experiments/D14_noslip_rest_rehearsal/SUMMARY.md`.
- **The contact-force check is circular.** For elliptic cones, `mj_contactForce` returns `efc_force` itself, so the
  check always gives 0, and it does not test the `efc_vel` rows. The Q4 creep share (reported only) rests on MuJoCo's
  documented row layout.
- **S1's reversed setup order is a small perturbation.** It changes piece poses by only about 0.02°, so the "not
  setup-linked" label tests sensitivity to a small perturbation, not the misalignment pattern itself.
